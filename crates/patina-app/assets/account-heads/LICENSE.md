# Offline account heads

`steve.png` and `alex.png` are original Patina pixel illustrations, drawn
from scratch on a 16×16 grid and enlarged to 64×64 with nearest-neighbor
sampling. They use grayscale only. The short dark hair and beard identify
the Steve-style head; the lighter side-swept hair and long side locks identify
the Alex-style head.
